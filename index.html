KEY ANALYTICAL FINDINGS
Frontier Airlines had the highest delay rate at 29.23% — nearly 8 points above the network average of 21.2%.
OBSERVED
Late Aircraft is the #1 BTS-reported delay category at 124M attributed minutes — larger than Carrier, NAS, Weather, and Security combined.
OBSERVED
Delay rate worsened every year: 20.51% in 2023 → 20.77% in 2024 → 22.25% in 2025. Consistent deterioration in on-time performance.
OBSERVED
MDW (Chicago Midway) had the highest total delay minutes and cancellation count — Southwest's primary hub under operational pressure.
INFERRED
Using Ferguson et al. $45/min assumption, total modeled delay cost exposure across 3 years is $14.95 billion.
MODELED
